📱 SMS & Messaging
Send automated appointment reminders and invoice notifications via text.
🏷️ Card Price
Dual pricing: card payments cost a little more than cash or check. Invoices show both, e.g. "Card price $154.50 / Cash or check $150.00". Changes apply to new invoices only.
💳 Stripe Payments
Connect Stripe to accept card payments on invoices. Your keys are stored securely. Connect Stripe or iPOSpays, not both.
Find these at dashboard.stripe.com/apikeys. A restricted key needs Write access to PaymentIntents, Checkout Sessions, Customers, PaymentMethods, Refunds and Webhook Endpoints.
💳 iPOSpays Payments
Take pay-link payments through your iPOSpays (Dejavoo) merchant account instead of Stripe. Connect one or the other, not both. While connected, Pay Now links and Card payments open an iPOSpays payment page. Refund them from the customer's Invoice History.
In the iPOSpays portal (Merchant Admin): API & secret key under Settings → Generate API & Secret Key; Ecom token under Settings → Merchant Keys / Ecom Token. Stored on FarriTech's server only.
